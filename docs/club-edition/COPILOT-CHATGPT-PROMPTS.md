# Lineup Card: optional prompts for Copilot or ChatGPT

Using an AI is optional. The Lineup Card works without it, and the AI never decides who is flagged.

The card already did that with fixed math, and you approved each flag on the review screen. These prompts only help you turn the approved card into words for staff.

## 1. Before you paste anything

- Use only the Copilot or ChatGPT your club has approved. Sign in with your club account.
- Follow your club's rules on player data. If you are not sure, replace each name with initials before you paste.
- Paste only text from the approved card. The AI never decides who is flagged. The card did that with fixed math, and you approved it.
- Read every answer before you send it. You are the one sending it.

## 2. Staff note from today's card

On the card, click "Copy for group text". Paste that text where the prompt says.

```text
Below is today's Lineup Card summary. A staff member has already reviewed and approved it.

Write a short note for the manager and staff. Rules:
- 4 to 6 lines. Plain words. No medical words.
- Keep every status word exactly as written: "Check in", "Watch", "No change", "Not enough data".
- "Check in" means: talk with him before he trains. "Watch" means: keep an eye on him today.
- Do not add any number, reason or advice that is not in the summary.
- Do not change who is on which list.
- End with: "Reviewed by [your name]"

Summary:
[paste the text from "Copy for group text" here]
```

## 3. Explain one flag to a coach

Copy one player's line from the card. For example, the line under "Check in today".

```text
Below is one player's line from today's Lineup Card. A staff member has already reviewed it.

Explain it to a coach in 3 sentences:
1. What changed today compared with his own normal.
2. What his status word means. "Check in" means: talk with him before he trains. "Watch" means: keep an eye on him today.
3. That coaches and medical staff decide who plays.

Rules:
- Use only what is in the line. Do not add numbers.
- No diagnosis. Do not say or suggest he is hurt, injured or sick.
- Keep the status word exactly as written.

Player line:
[paste one player's line here]
```

## 4. Weekly look-back

Paste the "Copy for group text" summary from 5 to 7 days, one after the other. Put the date at the top of each.

```text
Below are daily Lineup Card summaries from the past week. Each one was reviewed and approved by staff.

Make a short look-back:
- List players who were "Check in" or "Watch" on 2 or more days. For each, give the dates and the status word for each date.
- List the days when the summary has a line that starts "Whole team jumping" and says "below normal".
- Keep every status word exactly as written.

Rules:
- Use only what is in the summaries. Do not add numbers.
- No predictions. Do not say who will get hurt or who should rest.
- No diagnosis.

Summaries:
[paste each day's summary here, with its date]
```

## 5. What not to ask it

- Do not ask if a player is injured.
- Do not ask if a player should play or sit.
- Do not ask if a player will get hurt.
- Do not paste raw VALD, Catapult or wellness exports. Paste only the approved card text.
- Do not ask it to change a status. If you think a status is wrong, change it on the review screen and add a note.
